Terms of Service

Effective November 17, 2026 · Version française

1. Purpose

Genius Publication is a personal software installed on its owner's computer. It prepares short vertical videos and publishes them, at chosen times, to the YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts of that same owner, including Leonida Clips. It is not sold or offered to the public.

2. Users

Only the owner of the software signs in, with their own accounts. No registration is open to third parties and no other person's account is managed.

3. Platform connections

The software connects to each platform through its official access (Login with TikTok and Content Posting API, YouTube Data API, Instagram API) and only uses the permissions it needs:

  • read the account name and avatar to display them in the software;
  • upload a video with its title and caption, after the owner confirms each post and its settings;
  • read the statistics of published videos.

Each permission can be revoked at any time from the settings of the account concerned.

4. Published content

The owner is solely responsible for published videos. They only publish content they have the right to use, credit the original creator of each excerpt in the caption, and follow each platform's rules.

5. Availability

The software is provided as is, for personal use. A post may be delayed if the computer is off or if a platform temporarily refuses the upload.
